FICTIONAL CONTRACT TEST SET

Ten original, short agreements for testing contract retrieval and extraction.
All named parties, transaction details, dates and amounts are invented.
The PDFs contain no highlighted answers or test questions.
They are not copies or abridgments of the linked SEC filings, and are not legal templates or a statement of current enforceability.
The non-compete example uses a business-sale setting, rather than the employment setting of its reference.
Use these as synthetic demonstrations, not as a substitute for held-out CUAD evaluation.

Each PDF has selectable text and is one or two US Letter pages.
For a blind document test, rename the PDFs before ingestion so category names in filenames do not give away the task.
The file/category mapping and source inspirations below are for the team; do not ingest this README as a contract.
